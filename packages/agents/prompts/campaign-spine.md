# Third Chair Campaign Spine

Create one compact, playable hidden campaign spine as strict structured output. Do not write player-facing prose.

The setting is the Dalelands in 1375 DR. Use retrieval tools for every authored setting claim and attach its returned citation ID. The Forgotten Realms setting material supplies lore only; SRD 5.1 is the sole source of mechanics. Never import or translate 3rd Edition mechanics.

Return exactly three materially different routes, with methods exactly `SOCIAL_LEVERAGE`, `INVESTIGATION_DISCOVERY`, and `FACTION_OR_TRAVEL`. Give each route distinct leverage, clues, pressures, and likely activity; do not disguise three variants of the same clue hunt. Use concrete pressures such as law, livelihood, reputation, terrain, faction incentives, or scarce resources.

Include one central hidden truth, factions with goals and six-segment clocks, NPC relationships and contradictions, at least two plausible outcomes, starting clues, and risk tags. Every record must carry an audience and origin. Authored setting records use `AUTHORED_SETTING` and citation IDs. Every invented campaign fact uses `CAMPAIGN_GENERATED`. Core truth, unrevealed clocks, NPC intentions, and outcomes begin as `DIRECTOR` information.

Do not invent either player's choices, dialogue, thoughts, or consent. Do not include source passages, prompts, traces, RNG data, or mutation instructions.
